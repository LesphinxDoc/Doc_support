Thematic analysis by dictionary

Automatically analyze your verbatim by grouping lexicons (words and expressions) according to the themes specific to your study context. You can thus transform your textual contents into a list of quantifiable themes that you can summarize via a flat table, or cross with other variables in your study.

To do this:

  1. Click on the button.

  2. Select Textual & semantic analysis.

  3. Click Thematic analysis by dictionary.

  4. Select the text variable to be analyzed from the drop-down menu.

  5. Select a dictionary or start from scratch:

    • Start from scratch: manually create a new thematic grid.

    • Import a dictionary in *.dic, *.text or *.txt format by choosing the "Upload a dictionary" option.

    • Retrieve a thematic grid from another study.

    • Use a template available from the library.

  6. Click on the Load button (Close button if you selected Start from scratch).

The thematic analysis environment is composed of three distinct parts in its work area: the thematic structure, the list of records (verbatim) and the lexicon (words and expressions).

The thematic structure makes it possible to classify the observations of the corpus according to defined themes.

How to build your codebook?

From the thematic structure:

  1. Click + Add a category to add a category.

  2. Give a name to the new category created by clicking on the pencil.

  3. Add the themes associated with this category by clicking Add a theme.

    • The themes can then be organized by drag & drop using the button .

    • To delete a theme, click on the button .

  4. To add words to a theme, you can:

    Add the word manually by clicking on the + Add a word button or by dragging and dropping the word directly from the lexicon section.

    In order to facilitate the search of text, you can filter the contents of the lexicon by choosing the type of elements to display. To do this:

    1. Click the Filter button.

    2. Choose the items to display.

    You can choose to display only the lexicon and the verbatim that correspond to a certain profile. To do this:

    • Click on the button located at the top of the window.

    • Define the profile by selecting the variable(s) and modalities to be used.

      EXAMPLE: We only want to display the lexicon and verbatim of farmers aged 30-60.

  5. (optional) You can change the display type of the verbatim: sentences or observations and show/hide the orientations.

  6. Once you have finished building thematic dictionary, click OK.

    NOTE: The weight of the themes corresponds to the number of text fragments containing at least one word of the considered theme. These words come from the thematic dictionary used.

  • To save the themes as a new variable, click the Save themes button.
