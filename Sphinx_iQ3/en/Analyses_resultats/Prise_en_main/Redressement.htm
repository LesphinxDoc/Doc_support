Define an adjustment

Despite all the efforts deployed to select the individuals, during the phases of e-mailing for example, a sample may be "biased", which means that its composition is not satisfactory, as it doesn't correspond to the criteria of representativeness we have previously defined.

EXAMPLE: We wanted to reach 60% of women and 40% of men but this is actually not the case. It is then necessary to "adjust" the sample.

To do so:

  1. Navigate to the Calculation tab from the view properties and click Define a new adjustment.

  2. Click Add a criterion to select the variable on which you wish to adjust the sample.

  1. Indicate the percentage you want to obtain for each modality.

EXAMPLE: In our example, we have now 41.23% of women respondents as our objective is to have 60% of women surveyed. The goal is to make the sample representative of the population studied on this criterion.

  1. Click on the Next button.

  2. Give your variable a name.

  3. Click OK. A new adjustment variable will be created.

NOTE: It is possible to ignore the adjustment on some analyzes. To do this, select an analysis, and then check the "Ignore the adjustment" option from the Advanced tab.

NOTE: You can display the raw frequencies when you apply an adjustment to your view. To do this, from the properties of the view, calculation tab, check the always display the raw frequencies (unadjusted) box.
