DOWNLOAD DATA FROM THE ONLINE ACCOUNT

This section allows you to download data from your published survey to your SphinxOnline account.

To import data from your online account, proceed as follows:

  1. From your home panel, click on the Download study button.

  2. Log in to your online account:

    1. Select the Server URL (where your account is hosted).

      The drop-down menu allows you to select the server on which your survey will be published. By default, the SphinxOnline (www.sphinxonline.com) shared server is selected.

      If your server is not in the list:

      1. Click on the button .

      2. Select "Other server" from the drop-down menu.

      3. Then enter the address of your server.

      4. Click on Save to confirm.

      (Optional) The "Proxy..." button enables you to indicate your proxy information, gateway between your network and internet (Host, identifier and password). We recommend you contact your IT department to set up the proxy.

    2. Click on Next.

    3. SphinxOnline will open in your browser: if you are not yet logged in, enter your login details and click on Log in.

    4. A dialog box will open in your browser. Click Open Sphinx iQ3.

      Click on the image above for an animated preview

    5. Return to the Sphinx iQ3 application.

      NOTE: Connection to your online account from iQ remains active for a sliding period of 5 days: each new interaction with the server (publication, import, etc.) resets this period for a duration of 5 days from the action.

  3. If you have access to several accounts (personal account + shared accounts), select the account from which you want to download study data.

  4. Enter the name of the online study, then click Next.

  5. Choose the operation to perform:

    • Replace the Sphinx iQ survey and answer file with the SphinxOnline one: this option will allow you to replace your local data with those on the server. Variables not present in Sphinx iQ will be added.

      WARNING: By using this option, your local data will be lost.

    • Complete and update the survey and the Sphinx iQ response file: this option will merge your local data with the server data. Variables not present in Sphinx iQ will be added.

      NOTE: Only the data that are present on the server and not locally will be imported, thus keeping your local data.

  6. Click on Next.

  7. If there are views with identical names on the server and locally, you can:

    • Replace them (with the views present on the server) by clicking on the Replace local views with those of the server button.

    • Keep them by clicking on the Keep local views button.

  8. Click on Next.

    A window summarizing the download information (server name, study name, account name and action taken) will appear.

  9. Click on Finish to validate the operation.
