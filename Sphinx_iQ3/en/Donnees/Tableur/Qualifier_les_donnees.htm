QUALIFy Data

Data qualification is a functionality of Sphinx iQ3 that allows us to identify insufficiently documented or poorly filled-in variables, to distinguish singular observations and to replace non-responses.

EXAMPLE: Some respondents may answer the same thing over and over again on scale-type questions in order to complete the survey more quickly. The data set obtained may be biased, and it may be necessary to qualify the data in order to make it more relevant.

To qualify data, proceed as follows:

  1. From the Data tab of the Spreadsheet, click on the Qualify button.

  2. Select the operation you want to perform on your data set:

    • Identify singular or poorly documented observations: to identify observations insufficiently documented or very far from the average or systematically documented.

    • Replace non-responses: to replace the non-responses either by the average value (the most cited modality) or by the previous value.

    • Identify irrelevant variables: to identify variables insufficiently documented variables or having too little variance.

    • Extract a Qualified sample: to extract a subset of relevant variables and a Qualified sample for these relevant variables.

  3. (Optional) Click on Quality of the dataset to get a graphical overview of the relevance of your data.
