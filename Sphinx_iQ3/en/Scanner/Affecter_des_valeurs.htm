ASSIGN VALUES

The Assign Values function is used to give all scanned pages the same value for a variable.

EXAMPLE:
In the context of a questionnaire distributed during a training session, we would like to point out that all the observations that we have just scanned were for the "Analysis & Reporting" training conducted in Paris on April 12, 2019 (knowing that Training, Location, and Date questions are not displayed on the form that was administered).

Hide questions in Sphinx iQ3

To hide questions from the form, proceed as follows:

  1. Select the questions and right-click them.

  2. Click Hide from the form/Delete.

  3. A small window opens afterwards, click on the Hide button.

    Or click on the icon when hovering over the question.

NOTE: When you hide a question, it will no longer be displayed on the form but will remain in the survey.

To do so:

  1. From the Home menu of Sphinx Scan.

  2. Click on the Assign values button in the Post-processing group.

  3. Select a variable.

  4. Select the value (response) to assign to your observations (to all observations or only to selected observations).

  5. Click on OK to validate.
