How to edit the title of the study (Name of the Internet tab)?

To edit the title of the study, simply proceed as follows:

  1. From the Sphinx iQ3 home panel, click the icon (located to the right of the survey name), or click File, then Study Properties.

  2. Fill in the new title of the study.

  3. Click on OK to validate.

  4. Republish your survey.

The software asks me to enter a license key each time I open it, why?

If the software asks you to insert the key each time it starts, it means that you have not activated your license key in administrator mode.

To avoid this problem, you must launch the Sphinx iQ3 software as an administrator and then enter the license key.
