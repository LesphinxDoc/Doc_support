software Organization

The software program is organized according to the logic of study achievement: design, distribution, data preparation, and analysis of results.

After creating a new project or selecting an already existing one, the Home panel reveals, on opening, the five stages of the program : Questionnaire design, Distribution & collection, Data management and Result analysis. These stages give access to environments or assistants allowing to achieve various activities such as writing a questionnaire or data qualification.

All the environments are endowed with an icon allowing to switch back to the Home panel in order to activate other environments or reach other assistants.

HOME SCREEN

The first page that appears when the software is opened. It offers a set of headings (Create a study, Open a study, SphinxOnline).

In the case of a new project, it is possible to either create a study or import a database.

The "Recent projects" section in the Home tab shows the latest surveys opened in Sphinx iQ3. You can open them directly by clicking on their name.

NOTE: Note that it is possible to run the Sphinx iQ3 software several times, and thus open different surveys simultaneously.

This screen also allows you to access the SphinxOnline environment and all the functionalities it offers (emailing and follow-up of the distribution campaign).

HOME PANEL

The central panel that is displayed when a study project is opened. It offers four stages allowing, via environments or assistants, to design the questionnaire, to distribute the survey, to manage the data or to analyze the results.

  • The Questionnaire Design stage allows you to model the study via a diagram in order to synthesize its tree structure; to write the questionnaire by listing and setting all the questions that make it up; and finally to conceive the paper or screen form to communicate to respondents. This form may be based on modals either standard or customized.

  • The Distribution and Data Collection stage lets you publish the survey online and import the responses collected. It also lets you enter responses collected using paper forms or a scanner.

  • The Data Management stage allows you to consult the answers in their spreadsheet (modify, sort out and export if needed). We can set up a data qualification in order to identify the variables lacking documentation or information, distinguish particular observations, and replace the non-answers. This stage also allows you to weight the sample, change the statistical unit and calculate the new variables calculated from existing variables.

  • The Analysis of results stage allows the analysis of the answers and the preparation of analysis and reports. Customized graphical reports offer the setting of detailed and personalized analysis as well as the preparation of study reports which, after setting, could be exported to Microsoft Office (Word, Power Point…). Automatic graphical reports, the analysis of which are less elaborate, could be set up (evaluation of data quality or identification of tendencies). For thorough analysis, comparisons between samples, parts and variables as well as multivariate analyses are also possible.

ENVIRONMENT

A space composed of a working area, an horizontal strip and a lateral menu offering a lot of different functionalities.

  • The Questionnaire design stage offers two environments : Modeling my study to shape it into a diagram and Write my questionnaire to write questions and carry out the configurations. The first two environments are directly accessible from the home panel while the last one is available from the "Write my questionnaire" environment.

  • The Distribution and data collection stage proposes three environments : Data entry to enter the answers manually via an interface capable of optimizing the input time, Publish my survey to publish your survey online in your account SphinxOnline and Import data to download data and them to your survey locally.

  • The Data management proposes a Spreadsheet to consult data, modify them, sort them out…. It is also possible to qualify, redress and prepare data.

  • The Results Analysis stage allows you to create views with simple analyses (automatic analysis), prepare dashboards with customized analyses, perform more in-depth and specific analyses through the multivariate analysis environment and also analyze the content of your verbatim as well as their lexical composition through the text and semantic analysis environment.
