CREATE A new STUDY

To create a new study, proceed as follows:

  1. Click on the Create a Study button from the Home screen.

  2. Click on CREATE A STUDY FROM SCRATCH.

  3. Set the parameters of the study:

    • Title (present in the form banner).

    • (Optional) Comment, organization, period.

    • Templates and styles of the form.

    • Languages and translations.

      NOTE: These parameters can be modified at any time after the creation of your study.

  4. Click on Finish.

After creating your study, proceed to the design and setup of your questionnaire. More details in the Questionnaire section.
