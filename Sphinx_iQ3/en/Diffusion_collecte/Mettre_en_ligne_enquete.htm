PUBLISH / RE-PUBLISH THE STUDY

This section helps you to publish your survey online in your SphinxOnline account. This survey may therefore be disseminated to all your respondents, the collected responses will be also saved in your online account.

Publish the survey online is composed of two different sections, the case of a first publication and the case of publication of an already published survey in your SphinxOnline account.

How to get access?

  • From the home panel, click on the Publish the study button.

  • Or from the Questionnaire Design environment, Distribution tab, click on the Publish button.

PUBLISH THE STUDY

To publish a study to your online account, proceed as follows:

  1. Click on Publish.

  2. Log in to your online account:

    1. Select the Server URL (where your account is hosted).

      The drop-down menu allows you to select the server on which your survey will be published. By default, the SphinxOnline (www.sphinxonline.com) shared server is selected.

      If your server is not in the list:

      1. Click on the button .

      2. Select "Other server" from the drop-down menu.

      3. Then enter the address of your server.

      4. Click on Save to confirm.

      (Optional) The "Proxy..." button enables you to indicate your proxy information, gateway between your network and internet (Host, identifier and password). We recommend you contact your IT department to set up the proxy.

    2. Click on Next.

    3. SphinxOnline will open in your browser: if you are not yet logged in, enter your login details and click on Log in.

    4. A dialog box will open in your browser. Click Open Sphinx iQ3.

      Click on the image above for an animated preview

    5. Return to the Sphinx iQ3 application.

      NOTE: Connection to your online account from iQ remains active for a sliding period of 5 days: each new interaction with the server (publication, import, etc.) resets this period for a duration of 5 days from the action.

  3. If you have access to several accounts (personal account + shared accounts), select the account on which you want to publish the study.

  4. Enter the Survey name (this name will be visible in the URL you will get from this publication and will be visible to the respondents).

    NOTE: The study name is limited to 50 characters and must not contain any special characters or spaces.

    NOTE: The direct link of the questionnaire is composed as follows:
    servername/surveyserver/s/accountname/surveyname/shortcut.htm

  5. (Optional) Click on the Edit options button to set the publishing options.

  6. Click on Next.

  7. Select Publish the questionnaire and check the box Publish the local responses, if you already have answers and want to have them online.

    EXAMPLE: If your local questionnaire has 50 local responses, they will be published if you check this option. Otherwise, only the survey will be published on the server.

  8. Click on Next and check the publication information.

  9. Click on Finish.

    Once your survey has been published, a dialog box displays the links associated with the survey, including the different entry and modification forms.

    You can also access your SphinxOnline account, either by clicking on the link to your account manager, or by checking the option Direct access to the account manager, your account will open automatically from your internet browser when you click on OK.

  10. Click on OK to validate.

REPUBLISH THE STUDY

A survey published online can be republished at any time to update the forms, responses and analyses created. To republish a survey, proceed as follows:

  1. Click on Publish.

  2. Log in to your online account:

    1. Select the Server URL (where your account is hosted).

      The drop-down menu allows you to select the server on which your survey will be published. By default, the SphinxOnline (www.sphinxonline.com) shared server is selected.

      If your server is not in the list:

      1. Click on the button .

      2. Select "Other server" from the drop-down menu.

      3. Then enter the address of your server.

      4. Click on Save to confirm.

      (Optional) The "Proxy..." button enables you to indicate your proxy information, gateway between your network and internet (Host, identifier and password). We recommend you contact your IT department to set up the proxy.

    2. Click on Next.

    3. SphinxOnline will open in your browser: if you are not yet logged in, enter your login details and click on Log in.

    4. A dialog box will open in your browser. Click Open Sphinx iQ3.

      Click on the image above for an animated preview

    5. Return to the Sphinx iQ3 application.

      NOTE: Connection to your online account from iQ remains active for a sliding period of 5 days: each new interaction with the server (publication, import, etc.) resets this period for a duration of 5 days from the action.

  3. If you have access to several accounts (personal account + shared accounts), select the account on which you want to publish the study.

  4. Enter the Survey name to update.

  5. (Optional) Click on the Edit options button to set the publishing options.

  6. Click on Next.

  7. A message will appear telling you that the study already exists on the server. You should then choose between two options depending on the changes you have made:

    Replace the questionnaire on SphinxOnline with the one in Sphinx iQ3

    This option deletes the survey on the server and replaces it with the local questionnaire (forms, answers, analyses, etc.).

    Check the Clear the response file on SphinxOnline box to publish the survey without the local responses (responses on the online survey will be deleted).

    The "replace questionnaire" option is to be used when:

    • Deleting of modalities/ variables.

    • Modifying of the name of the variables.

      NOTE: If your survey is in progress, be sure to import the data (responses) from the online survey into your local survey before making changes and replacing the online survey.

    Update the questionnaire on SphinxOnline

    This option allows you to publish your local survey by merging it with the survey already present online. The elements of the local questionnaire that are not present in the online survey are added to it.

    NOTE: This option will always keep a maximum of information (modalities, questions, answers, analyses) about the online survey.

    Check the Modify the response file on SphinxOnline box if you want to complete the online answer file with the local answers.

    NOTE: If the same observation is present online and locally, the most recent response is retained (verification based on the value of the DATE_MODIF variable).

    The update option is to be used when:

    • Adding new modalities or new variables;

    • Modifying modality labels or variable labels;

    • Adding / modifying display conditions / references;

    • Modifying the form (formatting, addition / deletion of images, texts, etc...).

  8. Click on Next.

  9. If there are analysis views with identical names on the server, you can:

    • Replace them (with local views) by clicking on the Replace the views on SphinxOnline with those in Sphinx iQ button.

    • Keep them by clicking on the Keep the views that already exist on SphinxOnline button.

  10. Click on Finish to republish.
