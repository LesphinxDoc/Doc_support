Interpreting an analysis with AI

Make your data speak thanks to automated interpretation by AI.

Sphinx provides you with a written interpretation of your analysis in a matter of seconds, from the simplest (cross-tabulations) to the most advanced (AFC, ACP, K-means classification, CHD, multiple linear regression, etc.), generates a summary from a word cloud and much more. For more information about AI in our solutions, please consult Sphinx's website.

NOTE: Only data for the variable you have selected will be sent to AI for interpretation. No other data from your study will be used.

To interpret an analysis using AI:

  1. (Required) Log into your SphinxOnline account from Sphinx iQ3.

    Procedure for logging into the SphinxOnline account from Sphinx iQ3 :

    1. Open the Sphinx iQ3 software program.

    2. Click on Connection in the top right-hand corner of the screen.

    3. Select the Server URL (where your account is hosted).

      The drop-down menu allows you to select the server on which your survey will be published. By default, the SphinxOnline (www.sphinxonline.com) shared server is selected.

      If the server URL is not present in the list :

      1. Click on the + button.

      2. Select Other server from the Server drop-down menu.

      3. Insert the URL address of your server.

      4. Click on Save to validate.

    4. Click on Next.

    5. SphinxOnline will open in your browser: if you are not yet logged in, enter your login details and click on Log in.

    6. A dialog box will open in your browser. Click Open Sphinx iQ3.

      NOTE: Check the “Always allow [server URL] to open links of this type in the associated app” option to avoid displaying this window on subsequent logins.

    7. Return to the Sphinx iQ3 application.

      NOTE: Connection to your online account from iQ remains active for a sliding period of 5 days: each new interaction with the server (publication, import, etc.) resets this period for a duration of 5 days from the action.

    Once logged in, the account name appears in the top right-hand corner of the screen.

    By clicking on it, you can:

    • Open the online account in the browser.

    • Change account (if the personal account is linked to one or more collaborative accounts).

    • Log out.

  2. Select the analysis to be interpreted.

  3. Click on the icon .

    First time user? Accept the AI terms of use

    Acceptance of the general conditions specific to AI assistants is required.

    1. Read the specific terms and conditions for AI Assistants carefully.

    2. Check the following box:

      • "I acknowledge that I have accepted without reservation the ‘AI Assistant’ addendum displayed above and I declare that I am the legal representative of the holder of this account or have the authority to represent them validly and that I accept liability in the event of any false declaration".

    3. Click on the I accept button.

  4. (Recommended) Enter the context of the study so that the AI response is more precise.

    Procedure for entering a study context

    This context will be used for all automatic interpretations in this study.

    Click on the ‘click here’ link and fill in the following fields:

    • The subject / theme of my study is (maximum 500 characters).

      EXAMPLE: Tourism in the Annecy region.

    • The objectives of my study are (maximum 500 characters).

      EXAMPLE: To better understand my customers

    Do not communicate any sensitive information about your study subject or objectives.

  5. Click on the Result button.

    The interpretation of the analysis by AI is displayed.

    Please note that the interpretation is generated by artificial intelligence, which has its limitations: it may contain inaccuracies, biases and/or unethical content, and may vary from one interpretation to another. Human verification is strongly recommended.

  6. Click on Copy to clipboard to copy the interpretation.
