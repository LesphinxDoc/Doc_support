ACTIVATE A display CONDITION

This function consists in presenting a question/modality only if it corresponds to a chosen profile. Thus the presentation of the question/modality is conditioned by the answer to another question.

EXAMPLE: Display the question where we ask the respondent the make of their car only when they answer "Yes" to the question "Do you have a car at your disposal?".

NOTE: It is possible to apply presentation conditions on the rows/columns of a question table.

Display condition of a question

To set up a display condition on a question, do the following:

  1. Double-click on the question.

  2. Go to the Controls tab.

  3. Check the box Show only if....

  4. Click on Modify.

  5. Fill in the display condition.

Display condition of a modality

It is also possible to apply a display condition on the modalities of a question. Thus, the presentation of one or more modalities is conditioned by the answer to another question. To do this, proceed as follows:

  1. Double-click on the question.

  2. Go to the Controls tab.

  3. Click on the button from the Modalities section.

  4. Fill in the presentation condition to be used for each modality.

    NOTE: If you have many conditions, you can save time by generating the conditions according to the conditions of another variable. To do this, click on the "Generate from..." button.

    The Reset button allows you to delete all the conditions for the presentation of the terms of a question.

You can use a calculation formula to set a more complex display condition. To do this:

  1. Click on Multi-criteria profile.

  2. Select the Expression type from the drop-down menu.

  3. (Optional) Click on the Assisted Editor button to access the different calculation formulas.

  4. Fill in your calculation formula.
