Calculate a variable using AI

This feature allows you to calculate a new variable from one or more other variables by sending a specific request to the AI (write your AI prompt freely).

To access this feature, go to the Analysis section and, once you are in an analysis view, click on the Calculate a variable... button below the list of variables.

Then proceed as follows:

  1. Select the Calculate a variable by AI option.

    First time user? Accept the AI terms of use

    Acceptance of the general conditions specific to AI assistants is required.

    1. Read the specific terms and conditions for AI Assistants carefully.

    2. Check the following box:

      • "I acknowledge that I have accepted without reservation the ‘AI Assistant’ addendum displayed above and I declare that I am the legal representative of the holder of this account or have the authority to represent them validly and that I accept liability in the event of any false declaration".

    3. Click on the I accept button.

  2. Write your prompt in the provided area, choosing the variables to be used.

    A few tips:

    • You can double-click on a variable in the list of variables on the right to add it to your calculation formula.

    NOTE: To help you write your prompt, a dedicated page provides you with predefined templates and practical tips.

  3. Click Next.

    You will see a preview of your variable.

    EXAMPLE: Spell-check of a text variable:

    NOTE: Generating a preview of the results does not deduct AI tokens from your account.

  4. Click Next.

  5. Configure the variable to be created by entering its name (label) and type (numerical, text, code, etc.).

  6. (Optional) Select the Automatic variable check box so that the calculation is executed for any observation added or modified after the creation of the calculated variable.

  7. Choose where to insert the new variable.

  8. Click Finish.

Your AI-calculated variable is created.
