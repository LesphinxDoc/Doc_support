Conduct a multiple linear regression

The goal of multiple regression is to relate a variable, the variable explained with several other explanatory variables, in order to define the equation that connects them. We can thus, knowing the variables explained, determine the values of the explanatory variable: V1 = axV2 + bxV3 + cxV4. A multiple regression coefficient is then calculated. It indicates the quality of the fit performed by the model and is interpreted as a simple correlation coefficient.

In the case of a satisfaction survey, the overall satisfaction can be explained by relating it to the evaluation of the various attributes of the service in question. Partial regression coefficients (ai) provide an indication of how the perception of each attribute is involved in the formation of overall satisfaction.

The equation to which multiple linear regression leads allows both the relative importance of the different explanatory variables and the prediction of results. The variable to be explained and the explanatory variables are numbers (numerical variables or scales).


We try to explain the marks obtained in statistics, by 20 students according to three criteria: The number of hours of statistics courses taken, the score obtained in maths at the baccalaureate and the time, in number of hours, devoted to the revision.

In order to perform a multiple linear regression, proceed as follows:

  1. Click on the button.

  2. Select advanced analysis.

  3. Click on Multiple linear regression.

  4. Make a drag and drop of a variable to analyze and the explanatory variables.

Once the multiple linear regression is created, it is possible to change the variables by selecting the analysis and clicking or right clicking edit on the analysis.

MULTIPLE LINEAR REGRESSION OPTIONS

In the case of a multiple linear regression, there can be many variables which makes the graph's readability difficult. You can then zoom in on certain sections to see more closely an interesting area during the preview.

To zoom, select allow zoom in the chart elements.

In the preview, place the cursor on a part of the graph, click drag and make a square around the area to be zoomed. Then it will be possible to cancel the zoom by clicking on the button .

It is possible to display more analysis regarding the modalities of the analysis by selecting detailed results below the map. These can be exported to Excel by clicking on the button .

The statistical results can be displayed during the preview by selecting show "Detailed results" in runtime mode in the chart elements.
