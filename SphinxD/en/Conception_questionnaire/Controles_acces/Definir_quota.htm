Set entry quotas

Entry quotas are predetermined limits based on specific demographic or behavioral criteria. For example, you can limit the number of respondents by gender, age group, or geographic area. These quotas on response input help ensure that your sample accurately reflects your target population and reduces potential bias in the survey results.

To define entry quotas, proceed as follows:

  1. From the QUESTIONNAIRE tab, click on Quotas.

  2. Enter the target number (the total number of responses for your survey).

  3. Click on the Add a quota button.

  4. Select the variable you wish to use, then assign a number (or percentage) to its modalities.

    EXAMPLE: In our survey, we want to limit the number of participants to 40 men and 60 women. We will therefore define a quota of 100 responses, including 40 men and 60 men for our ‘gender’ variable.

    There are 3 buttons to help you set up a quota:

    : Resets the current quota values

    : Allows even distribution of the target number.

    : Delete the current quota.

    NOTE: The distribution must be equal to 100% of the total population defined.

  5. Check the Activate quotas on survey option.

  6. Click Save to confirm.

Quota actions

To access the quota actions, click on Actions on quotas.

  • You can choose to take into account observations during data entry when calculating quotas. To do this, simply tick the option Take into account ongoing entries being entered when calculating quotas.

  • By default, over quota observations are kept in the Data section. They can be identified on the basis of the Progression variable.

    If you don't want to keep them, simply uncheck the Save also over-quota responses (Quota Full and Screen Out) option.

  • Choose the action to be taken for each scenario:

    Display a message to the user explaining why they cannot reply or redirect them to a web page.

    • QuotaFull :

      The respondent matches the required profile, but the expected number of responses has already been reached. Their responses are not useful. They leave the questionnaire with an explanation as to why they cannot respond.

    • ScreenOut :

      The respondent does not match the required profile. Their responses are not useful. They leave the questionnaire with an explanation as to why they cannot respond.

    • Cas Complete:

      The respondent matches the required profile: they completed the questionnaire and their responses are taken into account.

      NOTE: Remember to write the messages in the different languages of your survey if the latter is multilingual.

Set up panelist redirection links Complete, QuotaFull and ScreenOut

First, you need to create a variable that takes the ID of your panelist provider.

This variable must be filled in:

  • Prior to the survey (by the panelist).

  • Beforehand by the panelist provider with its own parameters.

EXAMPLE:
- The variable created to take over the ID is called "idpanel".
- The parameter used by your panelist provider is called "ID".
- The link communicated to respondents will be as follows:
https://beta.sphinxonline.net/tiny/a/0zbt5sdj?idpanel=ID

The links to be used in the redirection in the ScreenOut, QuotaFull and Complete cases must be entered as follows:

EXAMPLE: https://xxxx?ID=[Idpanel]
xxxx : website address provided by the panelist provider.
ID: the parameter used by your panelist provider
Idpanel : the name of the variable which will contain the panelist ID.

NOTE: The name of the variable containing the panelist ID must be indicated in brackets (in our example, this is the ‘idpanel’ variable).

  1. Select the Redirection action.

  2. Then insert the link corresponding to each case.
