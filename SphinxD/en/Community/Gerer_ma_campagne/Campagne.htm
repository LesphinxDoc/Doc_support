Create a campaign

  1. Get to the Campaigns section, New Campaign tab.

  2. Enter the name of your campaign, the tags associated with it and a description in the different languages of your panel. 

    NOTE: You can choose these languages in the Community configuration and then in General.

  3. Select the survey to associate to this campaign.

    • Sphinx Survey: Select the associated survey from the list of surveys available in your account.

    • External Activity: Enter the URL of your survey created with an external tool in the External URL field.

      Your external survey must redirect respondents to Community at the end of the questionnaire.

      Configuring Callback URLs for an External Survey

      Your external survey must redirect respondents to Community at the end of the questionnaire.

      • Basic URL (without quota management): Use this option if you are not managing quotas in your survey:

        • Required callback URL

      • Advanced URLs (with quota management): Use this option if your external survey manages quotas:

        • URL for Survey completed ;

        • URL for Quota Full;

        • URL for Screened Out.

    NOTE: You can automatically assign a value to certain variables in your questionnaire using the additional query parameters.

  4. Set up your campaign's properties:

    • Response type:

      • Unique: the member is authorized to respond only once to the survey;

      • Unique and modifiable: the member is authorized to modify their answer as many times as desired;

      • Unique and anonymous: respondent credentials will not be saved in the survey.

    • Incentive: assign the number of points that the member will earn by responding to the questionnaire.

      NOTE: The respondent must save their responses to receive their reward.

    • Opening: open, close or set an opening period for the campaign during which members will be able to respond to the questionnaire.

    • Registration: automatically register new members to this campaign and/or prevent members enrolled in this campaign from enrolling in another campaign until they have completed the questionnaire.

      NOTE: It is possible to block respondents for x days: members who are registered to this exclusive campaign cannot be selected for a new registration for X days after completing the questionnaire.

  5. Select the action to execute after a member has responded and saved their answers:

    • Display the survey thank you message;

    • Display a custom message;

    • Redirect to an URL;

    • Redirect to the form;

    • Redirect to the member's area.

  6. Click on Create to confirm the creation of the campaign.
