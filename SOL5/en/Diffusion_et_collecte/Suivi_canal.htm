Sending statistics of an Email/SMS channel

You can access the detailed monitoring of an Email/Sms channel at any time after sending your invitations/reminders, with the indicators there updated in real time.

To access detailed monitoring, proceed as follows:

  1. From the Distribution tab, All channels section.

  2. Select the relevant Email/Sms channel.

  3. Click on the Sending statistics tab.

    Several indicators are displayed:

    • Number of recipients defined.

    • Number of recipients contacted (with details of the number of emails / sms sent).

      • Complete response: The person has fully completed the questionnaire by validating their entry.

      • Abandoned during data entry: This refers to recipients who have gone beyond the first page without completely finishing their response (without validating their answers).

      • Immediate abandonment: These are recipients who have left the questionnaire on the first page.

      • Entry in progress: These are recipients who are currently answering the questionnaire.

        NOTE: If a recipient starts to fill in the questionnaire but stops without completing it, the action will be counted under the ‘Abandon during data entry’ indicator after 30 minutes of inactivity. However, if the recipient clicks on the questionnaire link again, the action will again be counted under the ‘Entry in progress’ indicator.

      • Click on the response link: Number of people who clicked on the questionnaire link in the email sent.

      • Undeliverable: This is the number of recipients whose email could not be delivered (e.g. non-existent address, mailbox full, etc.).

    • Number of recipients on the blacklist: These are recipients who have clicked on the unsubscribe link or who have sent the STOP message in the case of SMS messages.

    • Number of recipients not contacted: These are recipients who have not yet been contacted (scheduled or draft).

Recipient status

The status of recipients can be viewed from the list of recipients in an Email/SMS channel.

  • Not contacted: The recipient has not yet been contacted.

  • Contacted: A message has been sent to the recipient.

  • Currently answering: The recipient is currently answering the questionnaire.

    NOTE: If a recipient starts to fill in the questionnaire but stops without completing it, their status will automatically change to ‘Having given up’ after 30 minutes of inactivity. If the recipient clicks on the questionnaire link again, the status will revert to ‘Currently answering’.

  • Having answered: The recipient has fully responded to the questionnaire by validating their input.

  • Having given up: The recipient has abandoned the questionnaire.

  • Undeliverable: The e-mail could not be delivered (e.g. non-existent address, mailbox full, etc.).

  • Blacklisted: the email address or telephone number is blacklisted, so no messages can be sent to it.

Sending status

  • Sent: this status indicates that the email has been sent by Le Sphinx but has not necessarily been received by the user (it may be set to hard bounce, soft bounce).

  • Delivered: delivered emails have been sent successfully and have been accepted by the recipient's server. They can be filed in the recipient's inbox or in their spam box.

  • Clicked : This status allows you to determine that the recipient has clicked on at least one of the links in the email but not necessarily on the survey link. For example, there may be several links in the message such as the unsubscribe link or the read link if the recipient has difficulty viewing the message.

  • BadDomain : this status indicates that the domain of the address entered is invalid.

  • BadFormat : this status indicates that the format of the email address is invalid : absence of the "@" in the address for example.

  • Hard Bounce : this status indicates that there is a permanent error, the email cannot be delivered (non-existent address), the Sphinx will not try to send the email again

  • Soft Bounce : this status indicates that there is a temporary problem that prevents access to the mailbox. The Sphinx will automatically retry to send this email a few hours after it was first sent.
