Perform a Tetraclasse analysis (Llosa matrix)

The Llosa matrix consists in classifying the factors that may act on consumers satisfaction-dissatisfaction couple with regard to a service in a general way according to two criteria: the level of factors performance and their influence on the overall satisfaction.

In order to perform a Tetraclass analysis, proceed as follows:

  1. Click on the button.

  2. Select advanced analysis.

  3. Click on Tetraclasse - Llosa matrix.

  4. Choose the variables that correspond to partial satisfaction and overall satisfaction.

  5. (optional) Rename attribute groups and axes if needed.

    By default, this matrix makes it possible to release 4 groups of attributes: the basics elements, keys, plus and secondaries.

  6. Click on Next.

  7. Dichotomize the variables of overall satisfaction and partial satisfaction so as to have two modalities: satisfied and unsatisfied.

    Click on apply to all similar variables to dichotomize the rest of the similar variables in the same way.

Once the matrix is created, it is possible to change the variables by selecting the analysis and clicking or right clicking edit on the analysis.

PRESENTATION OPTIONS AND MATRIX ANALYSIS

In the options tab, chart category , it is possible to select size proportional to frequencies. Thus, the bubbles size will be proportional to the studied variables size.

The matrix is composed of four quadrants:

  • Quadrant of the Plus elements (excellence factors): groups the factors that strongly contribute to satisfaction when they are perceived positively; weakly to dissatisfaction when they are perceived negatively.

  • Quadrant of Key elements (driving factors): includes criteria that have a strong influence on satisfaction when they are perceived positively and on dissatisfaction when they are perceived negatively.

  • Quadrant Basic elements (essential factors): contains criteria that strongly contribute to dissatisfaction when they are perceived negatively and weakly to satisfaction when they are perceived positively.

  • Quadrant of Secondaries elements (neutral factors): groups the factors that exert a weak influence on the satisfaction whatever the perception.

In the case of a Llosa matrix, there can be many variables which makes the graph's readability difficult. You can then zoom in on certain sections to see more closely an interesting area during the preview.

To zoom, select allow zoom in the chart elements.

In the preview, place the cursor on a part of the graph, click drag and make a square around the area to be zoomed. Then it will be possible to cancel the zoom by clicking on the button .
