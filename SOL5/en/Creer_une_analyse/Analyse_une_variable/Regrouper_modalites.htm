Group modalities

In the case of an analysis containing variables of closed or scaled types, it is possible to group the modalities of a qualitative variable.

EXAMPLE: In a tourism survey and in the case of an analysis of the type of accommodation (hotel, family / friends, rental / lodging, camping) we may want to group them in « Premium » and « Low-cost » to have a more synthetic analysis reading.

To group modalities, do the following:

  1. Select your analysis.

  2. In the options tab, calculation category, check the Answer options management box,.

  3. Click the button .

  4. Click on the Add category button to add a new category.

    NOTE: You can select groups that you have already made from the Select existing groups ... drop-down menu .

  5. Move modalities by drag & drop to order / edit categories.

  6. (optional) You can hide/exclude modalities:

    • Excluded modalities: These modalities are hidden and ignored in the calculations.

    • Hidden modalities: These modalities are not displayed but they are taken into account in the calculations.

  7. Click OK to validate.

  8. (optional) Check the option detail categories if you want to detail the content of the created categories.

In the case of a cross-analysis containing one or more scaled or closed variables type, it is possible to choose the variable on which the modalities grouping will be effective through the drop-down menu and if you want to apply it to all similar variables.
