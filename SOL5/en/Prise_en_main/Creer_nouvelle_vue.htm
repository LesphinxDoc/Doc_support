Create/edit a view

To create a new view, proceed as follows:

  1. Get to the Analysis section.

  2. If you created at least one analysis view, click the Create a new view button. Otherwise, skip to step 3.

  3. Select your view format.

    Dashboard, 16:9 Report, A4 Report and Infographic

    When you select the Dashboard, 16:9 Report, A4 Report or Infographic format, the size of your analysis view is already set.

    You can select a formatting template that will apply a predefined color scheme, font, and layouts.

    Custom view

    When you select a custom view to view your data, you can customize the size (in pixels) of your analysis view.

    You can also decide whether or not to enable Responsive Design.

    NOTE: When Responsive Design is enabled, the view display adapts to the size of the screen or tablet. The exact placement of the analyses is then not retained. This option also applies to mobile display if you have not created a specific mobile view for that device.

  4. Name your analysis view.

  5. (Optional) Edit the default reference font and/or the color theme of your analysis views and adapt them to your company's branding.

    Customize the analysis view color theme

    You can choose the type of color palette to apply to your analyses:

    • One color, with or without gradient;

    • Two colors, with or without gradient;

    • A color palette:

      • An existing color can be deleted when hovering over it and clicking the trash icon at the top right.

      • You can add ad many colors as you want by clicking + on the right of existing colors.

      • If you set a custom color palette, you can save it for ulterior use by clicking the star icon.

    NOTE: Preset color palettes are available by clicking the palette icon on the right of the drop-down menu. 

    You can change colors at any time by clicking on them and then moving the slider on the palette or entering a hexadecimal code. Once changed, you can save a color in your favorite colors.

  6. Click OK.

Your analysis view is now created.

NOTE: When creating a new analysis view, you can View advanced options by clicking on the link button at the bottom left of the modal to configure your view in more detail, then Back to simple mode. You can find/modify the settings you configured when creating your new view in the View Properties once it has been created.

Advanced options

As soon as you create your new analysis view or after you have finalized its creation, you can configure the advanced options for your view.

GENERAL TAB

In the General tab, you can:

  • Enter a name for the view.

  • Describe what the view is, what it contains.

  • Checking the password free access box allows you to disable the restricted access to the view, to insert a password, click control access.

  • Check the Allow view export box to allow users to download the view in the following formats : PDF (Adobe Reader), CSV (Excel), or PPT (PowerPoint).

CALCULATION TAB

In the Calculation tab, you can:

  • Choose to limit the view to the profile. This allows you to designate the modalities or values of one or more variables that will be applied as a filter to the entire view.

  • Choose the adjustment variable. This is a variable that must already be present in the survey. This one is going to change the representative sample to match your needs because its composition doesn’t meet the desired selection criteria.

  • Choose to round the frequencies of your values.

  • Definition of significance thresholds, this function allows you to modify the thresholds used in the tests of significance of the whole customized graphical report. Type the values of thresholds wished, then validate.

  • Highlight significant values: all values, only overrepresented values, or only underrepresented values.

  • Display statistical test results for each analysis.

  • Choose whether to ignore non-responses to show or hide them in tables and charts.

DISPLAY TAB

In the Display tab, you can:

  • Choose the size of the visualization according to which the different pages of the view will appear, depending on the restitution format: on paper or on screen.

  • Enable the Responsive Design in runtime mode. When this option is enabled, the view display adjusts to fit the size of the screen or tablet. The exact placement of the analyzes is then not kept. This option also applies to mobile display if you have not created a specific view for this device.

  • Choose the variable titles. During the view edition the variable will be named by default according to their names, labels or their aliases.

  • Choose to Show sample information. This allows you to show on the view what kind of population is analyzed.

  • Apply cascading filters: the selection of a filter impacts the content of the filters located to its right.

  • Choose to Highlight filtered analysis by displaying the analyzed population below the filtered analysis.

  • Show left and right navigation buttons.

  • Change the position of the pagination in runtime mode: at top or at bottom.

  • Select the number of decimals for numerical values and percentages.

COLORS TAB

You can choose the type of color palette to apply to your analyses:

  • One color, with or without gradient;

  • Two colors, with or without gradient;

  • A color palette:

    • An existing color can be deleted when hovering over it and clicking the trash icon at the top right.

    • You can add ad many colors as you want by clicking + on the right of existing colors.

    • If you set a custom color palette, you can save it for ulterior use by clicking the star icon.

NOTE: Preset color palettes are available by clicking the palette icon on the right of the drop-down menu. 

You can change colors at any time by clicking on them and then moving the slider on the palette or entering a hexadecimal code. Once changed, you can save a color in your favorite colors.

You can also:

  • Choose the background color of the page.

  • Choose the background color of the view.

  • Applying a border to the view.

STYLE TAB

In the Style tab, you can:

  • Choose the reference font and the color.

  • Choose for analysis, graphs, charts and shapes, font, size and color of different text boxes such as statistical tests, axes or labels for example.

  • Choose the display style for significant values: Binary style (customizable colors) or Style differentiated by significance level (fixed colors).

  • Apply a colored layer to your images, under Image.

  • Edit the appearance of the view's styles (font, size, color, formatting), under Text.
